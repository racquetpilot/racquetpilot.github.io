Common questions

The reading doesn't register

In a quiet place, tap the middle of the stringbed a few times with a finger or a screwdriver handle. Tapping the frame or the edge picks up a different frequency.

The reading is lower than what I asked the stringer for

That's normal. Stringbed tension right after stringing is below the machine's pull. What we track is "now versus freshly strung."

How accurate is it?

During the beta, readings are for reference. Measured the same way on the same racquet, the trend is reliable; absolute values improve as calibration data accumulates.

I want my data deleted

In the app, Settings (⚙) → Withdraw and delete my data deletes it immediately. You can also email us. Details are in the privacy policy.
